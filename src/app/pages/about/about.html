FANDOMVERSE • ABOUT

About Us

FandomVerse is a responsive single-page fandom portal bringing anime, gaming, movies, TV shows, K-Pop, comics and manga together.

Our Goal

Make fandom discovery simple by bringing profiles, media, articles, events, releases and merchandise into one organized experience.

Technology

Angular, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, JSON data and browser storage are used for this no-backend project.

Team

A four-member development team divides layout, categories/data, search/media and interactive features.
